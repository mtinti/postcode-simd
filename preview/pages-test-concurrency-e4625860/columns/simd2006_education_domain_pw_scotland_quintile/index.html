    simd2006_education_domain_pw_scotland_quintile¶

    SIMD 2006 education domain, Scotland quintile, 1 = most deprived. Computed population-weighted band, using a midpoint rule validated against PHS's published overall bands; equal ranks grouped together; not published by PHS or the Government

    What the education domain measures (indicators in the Scottish Government's SIMD 2020v2 glossary; earlier editions' indicators differ in detail):

    • School pupil attendance (EduAttend, percentage; SIMD 2020v2 glossary: a 2020v2 example, not verified for this edition)
    • Attainment of school leavers (EduAttain, score; SIMD 2020v2 glossary: a 2020v2 example, not verified for this edition)
    • Working age people with no qualifications (EduNoQuals, standardised ratio; SIMD 2020v2 glossary: a 2020v2 example, not verified for this edition)
    • Proportion of people aged 16-19 not participating in education, employment or training (EduNotPart, percentage; SIMD 2020v2 glossary: a 2020v2 example, not verified for this edition)
    • 17-21 year olds entering university as a proportion of the 17-21 population (EduUniver, percentage; SIMD 2020v2 glossary: a 2020v2 example, not verified for this edition)

    Scope: every data zone of SIMD 2006, once, with its population; never the table's rows.

    What was done to it: computed. Zones ordered by the domain rank, equal ranks one block; band = max(1, min(5, ceil(midpoint x 5 / total))), where midpoint is the cumulative population through the block minus half the block's. Computed, not published. (code: simd_ingest/core/weighted.py: population_bands)

    How it is checked (what each check tests; whether a build passed it is in that build's manifest.json["checks"])

    • weighted.2006.zones: the population is joined to every PHS zone of the edition
    • weighted.2006.reproduces_phs_scotland_quintile: the population midpoint rule reproduces PHS's published overall Scotland band on every zone
    • join.<table>.simd2006_education_domain_pw_scotland_quintile.range: every joined band is within its range
    • readback.<table>.attached_values: every attached value in the saved file is looked up again from the sources and equal
